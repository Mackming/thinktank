# ThinkTank Showcase & Landing Page

A bespoke, zero-dependency, single-file interactive landing hero for ThinkTank, crafted with mathematical wave physics, parchment & ink aesthetics, and a single-take transition between the Horizon and the Articles of Deliberation.

## Deploying to Vercel in 1 Minute

1. Go to [vercel.com/new](https://vercel.com/new).
2. Select your `thinktank` repository.
3. In the project setup screen:
   * **Root Directory:** Click `Edit` and select `landing`.
   * **Framework Preset:** `Other` (or leave default).
   * **Build Command:** Leave empty (static site).
   * **Output Directory:** Leave empty.
4. Click **Deploy**.

Your showcase will be live instantly on your Vercel URL!
